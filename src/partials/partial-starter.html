Body Text & Readability

This is lead paragraph text (1.25rem) - perfect for introductory content that needs emphasis without being a heading. It draws readers into your content with comfortable sizing and spacing.

Regular body text maintains excellent readability at 18px base size with a relaxed 1.7 line-height. This combination creates comfortable reading rhythms and reduces eye strain during longer reading sessions. The slightly larger base size ensures accessibility across devices and accommodates users who may have difficulty reading smaller text.

Manrope's geometric construction gives it a modern, technical feel that aligns with your role as a Creative Technologist. Yet its subtle humanist touches keep it warm and approachable - not cold or sterile like pure geometric typefaces.

The font includes features like proper kerning, multiple weights (we're using 400, 600, 700, and 800), and excellent unicode support. It's optimized for screen display while maintaining a distinct personality. Links maintain clear affordance with color, underline, and adequate click/tap targets.

Small text (0.875rem / 14px) for captions, metadata, or supplementary information. Still readable but visually subordinate to main content.

Why Manrope?

Not Overused

While clean and professional like Helvetica Neue, Manrope isn't ubiquitous. It gives your site a fresh, contemporary feel without following trends.

Excellent Variable Weights

The font family offers weights from 200 to 800, giving you flexibility for emphasis and hierarchy without font switching.

Screen-Optimized

Manrope was designed for digital interfaces, with careful attention to rendering at various sizes and pixel densities.

Geometric with Soul

It bridges the gap between pure geometric typefaces (like Futura) and humanist sans-serifs, giving you the best of both worlds - clean lines with subtle organic warmth.
