Privacy Policy

Last updated: Feb 2, 2026

The Short Version: I don't collect, store, or sell your personal information. This site is my portfolio—I just want to show you my work. The only data collected comes from basic analytics (to see if anyone actually visits) and third-party services like YouTube embeds.

What Information I Collect

Directly from you

Nothing, unless you use a contact form to email me. In that case, I receive your name, email address, and message—but I don't store it in a database. It just sends me an email.

Automatically

If I use analytics (like Plausible, Fathom, or Simple Analytics), I collect basic traffic data:

  • Pages you visit
  • How long you stay
  • General location (city/country level, not your exact address)
  • Device type (desktop, mobile, etc.)

These privacy-focused tools don't use cookies and don't track you across other websites. They just tell me if people are looking at my work.

Third-Party Services

Google Fonts

I use Google Fonts to make the site look nice. When you load the site, your browser requests these fonts from Google's servers. Google's Privacy Policy

YouTube

If I embed videos, they're loaded from YouTube (using privacy-enhanced mode when possible). YouTube may set cookies and collect data. YouTube's Privacy Policy

CodePen

Some projects are embedded from CodePen. They may collect basic usage data. CodePen's Privacy Policy

CDNs (Content Delivery Networks)

I use CDNs to load JavaScript libraries faster. These are just file delivery services—they don't track you.

Data Stored on Your Device

LocalStorage (Game Scores)

If you play any mini-games on this site, your scores are saved in your browser's local storage—on your device, not my server. I never see this data. You can clear it anytime by clearing your browser data.

Security

This site uses HTTPS encryption (via SiteGround) to protect any data transmitted between your browser and my server. But honestly, there's not much to protect since I'm not collecting anything.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Ask what data I have about you (spoiler: probably none)
  • Request deletion of any data
  • Opt out of analytics by using browser extensions or privacy settings

Since I'm not storing your data, there's not much to delete. But if you email me and want that conversation deleted, just let me know.

External Links

My site links to other websites (like LinkedIn, project demos, client work). I'm not responsible for their privacy practices—check their policies if you're concerned.

Changes to This Policy

If I change how I handle privacy (like adding new features), I'll update this page and note the date at the top.

Questions?

Email me: contact@heathernew.com
Or find me on LinkedIn

That's it. No legalese, no hidden tracking, no selling your data. Just a portfolio site.
