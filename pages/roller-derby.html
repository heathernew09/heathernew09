Award Ceremony

I wanted to recognized exceptional dedication to the league by Dogpile. She started with the league in 2006, she became a welcoming face to newbie skaters. I designed and crafted an oversized "Epic Medal" for teammate to resemble her loyalty. It was made out of carved, cut and layered plywood, copper for the ring, and Jo "Chewblocka" sewed the giant ribbon using a satin vinyl. I applied layers of paints, metallic glitters and shadows.
