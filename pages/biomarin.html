Race Against the Plates

An engaging "Beat the Clock" interactive experience designed for tradeshow booths. Making complex medical information about bone growth plates accessible and memorable for healthcare professionals.

Technology
HTML5 / JS
Platform
Touchscreen Kiosk
The Challenge

The Window of Opportunity.

With Achondroplasia, there is only a limited window of time to act while bones are still growing. The challenge was to communicate this urgency through a competitive, high-pressure interactive experience that would stand out in a busy scientific exhibit.

  • → Simplify complex growth plate biology
  • → Create high-engagement booth activation
  • → Drive knowledge retention through competition
  • → Enable rapid testing of medical expertise
Interaction Design

The Mechanics of Time

Custom SVG components built to create tension and urgency. This 30-second countdown mechanic was integrated into the 32" kiosk experience to drive competitive play and ensure a high throughput of users at the booth.

Visual Storytelling

Gamifying Scientific Data

Using custom SVG animations and a "Race Against Time" mechanic, we transformed abstract clinical data into a tactile, responsive experience that HCPs could interact with directly on the tradeshow floor.
