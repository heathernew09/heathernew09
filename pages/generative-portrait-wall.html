01 — The Algorithmic Design

Automating the Art Director

I designed a variable grid system that dynamically composes user portraits in real-time. By mapping specific user answers to tagged image assets, the system generates a unique piece of brand art for every user — instantly formatted for an 85-inch 4K display.

No two portraits are the same. Every one looks intentional. The grid doesn't just hold images. It thinks.

04 — Security by Design

Enterprise Maturity

This is the part most creative technologists don't think about. I do.

HIPAA / HiTrust Compliant

No Direct Attachments.
Ever.

In the healthcare space, data privacy is paramount. I architected the email delivery system to use expiring download links rather than direct image attachments — ensuring user data was never at risk, and the system met HiTrust compliance requirements from day one.

Links expire after two weeks. User data stays protected. Always.

05 — Brand Translation

User-Generated.
Still On Brand.

The challenge: how do you make user-generated content look on-brand? By building a strict visual framework around the agency's core attributes, we ensured that even user-created collages felt like polished agency marketing material.
