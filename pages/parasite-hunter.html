Team & Technology

Refining the Experience

George Bird had a grand vision to extend our reach! Translating 2D comic aesthetic into performant 3D WebAR required rigorous oversight. As Product Designer for UX and UI, I explored the capabilities of the tech and identified different friction points while working through the various stages of communicating the flow and plan.

  • Team Diligence: Our team was very diligent about how the technology interacts in the space.
  • Input Sensitivity: Raycasting adjustments for snappy parasite captures
  • Asset Performance: 3D model optimization with video team and vendor

All of the edits were done before we hit the show floor. A 6-month sprint. Long days, technical hurdles — and what leadership called Elanco’s "best VMX ever."
