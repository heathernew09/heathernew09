The Zen Garden
Decision Tree

Inside 1.2M sq. ft. of trade show, we built a quiet corner. Booth attendees — waiting in line or working on crafts — could interact with a branching decision tree that walked them through real dermatology options their vet can provide. Three pathways. One clear outcome.
