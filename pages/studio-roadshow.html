Know the drill?

Hey everyone! We all know the drill, right? RGB for digital, CMYK for print.

Hex codes, spot colors, client brand guides... making sure that perfect 'Crimson Fire' red doesn't turn into 'Muddy Maroon' when it hits the press. It's crucial, it's detailed... and let's be honest, sometimes it feels like a pop quiz we didn't study for.

What if we could turn that daily color challenge into a game?
