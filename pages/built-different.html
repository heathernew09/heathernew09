Innovation

Even our errors are Built Different.

404 Error pages usually result in a 100% bounce rate. We turned a dead end into a brand moment by creating "Flappy M", a physics-based browser game using raw JavaScript that keeps users engaged even when they're lost.

Cultural Architecture (ERG)

Inclusion in Motion

MERGE has 9 ERGs including: Caregivers, Interfaith, Generational, LGBTQIA+, Military, POC, Women in Tech, Remote, and Wellness. These ERGs have different initiatives in MERGE but we all have a collective mission to uphold Diversity, Equality and Inclusion. I designed a system for modeling each group, bold colors, simple symbolism, M to tie us together. The interaction below became a quick idea exploration to show them to you. It is using physics-based interaction models that I feel conceptually fit our ecosystem. Click the logo coins below to interact. Hold to move. Mouse to the far right to keep scrolling down the page.
